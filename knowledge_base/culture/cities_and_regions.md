# German Cities and Regions (Städte und Regionen)

## Germany in numbers
- Population: about 83 million – the largest country in the European Union by population.
- Capital: Berlin.
- 16 federal states (Bundesländer). Three of them are city-states: Berlin, Hamburg and Bremen.
- Neighbouring countries (9): Denmark, Poland, Czech Republic, Austria, Switzerland, France, Luxembourg, Belgium, Netherlands.
- Highest mountain: Zugspitze (2,962 m) in the Bavarian Alps.
- Longest river within Germany: the Rhine (der Rhein). Other important rivers: Danube (Donau), Elbe, Main.
- German is also an official language in Austria, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg and Belgium.

## Major cities
### Berlin
- Capital and largest city (about 3.7 million people).
- Sights: Brandenburger Tor (Brandenburg Gate), Reichstag building with its glass dome (seat of the Bundestag), East Side Gallery (the longest remaining section of the Berlin Wall, covered in murals), Museum Island (Museumsinsel, a UNESCO World Heritage Site), Checkpoint Charlie, Alexanderplatz with the TV tower (Fernsehturm).
- Known for its startups, nightlife, art scene and the Currywurst.

### Hamburg
- Second-largest city, with one of Europe's largest ports.
- Sights: the Elbphilharmonie concert hall, the Speicherstadt warehouse district (UNESCO), the Reeperbahn, the fish market on Sunday mornings.
- The Beatles played in Hamburg clubs before they became famous.

### München (Munich)
- Capital of Bavaria, third-largest city.
- Home of the Oktoberfest, BMW, Siemens and the football club FC Bayern München.
- Sights: Marienplatz with the Glockenspiel, Englischer Garten (one of the largest city parks in the world), the Frauenkirche, Neuschwanstein Castle is about two hours away.

### Köln (Cologne)
- The Cologne Cathedral (Kölner Dom) is a UNESCO World Heritage Site; construction began in 1248 and was finished in 1880.
- Famous for carnival and the beer "Kölsch", served in small 0.2-litre glasses.
- Eau de Cologne (Kölnisch Wasser) perfume comes from here.

### Frankfurt am Main
- Financial centre of Germany: home of the European Central Bank and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange. Its skyline gives it the nickname "Mainhattan".
- Frankfurt Airport is the biggest airport in Germany.
- Birthplace of the writer Johann Wolfgang von Goethe.

### Other important cities
- **Stuttgart** – home of Mercedes-Benz and Porsche.
- **Dresden** – baroque city on the Elbe, the rebuilt Frauenkirche, called "Florence on the Elbe".
- **Heidelberg** – Germany's oldest university (founded 1386) and a famous castle ruin.
- **Nürnberg (Nuremberg)** – medieval old town, Christmas market, Lebkuchen.
- **Leipzig** – Johann Sebastian Bach worked here; the 1989 Monday demonstrations started here.
- **Bonn** – capital of West Germany 1949–1990; birthplace of Ludwig van Beethoven.
- **Wolfsburg** – headquarters of Volkswagen.

## Regions and landscapes
- **Schwarzwald (Black Forest)** – mountain forest in Baden-Württemberg, known for cuckoo clocks and the Black Forest cake.
- **Bavarian Alps** – mountains in the south, with Zugspitze and many ski resorts.
- **North Sea and Baltic Sea coasts** (Nordsee und Ostsee) – islands like Sylt and Rügen; the Wadden Sea (Wattenmeer) is a UNESCO site.
- **Rhine Valley** (Mittelrheintal) – castles and vineyards along the river, the Loreley rock.
- **Romantische Straße** – a famous tourist route through medieval towns like Rothenburg ob der Tauber.

# Articles and Noun Gender (der, die, das)

Every German noun has a grammatical gender: masculine (der), feminine (die) or neuter (das). Gender is part of the word and must be learned together with the noun. All German nouns are written with a capital letter.

## Definite and indefinite articles (Nominativ)

| Gender    | Definite | Indefinite | Negative | Example          |
|-----------|----------|------------|----------|------------------|
| Masculine | der      | ein        | kein     | der Tisch (table)|
| Feminine  | die      | eine       | keine    | die Lampe (lamp) |
| Neuter    | das      | ein        | kein     | das Buch (book)  |
| Plural    | die      | –          | keine    | die Bücher       |

In the plural, all genders use the article "die".

## Rules of thumb for guessing gender

These patterns cover many nouns, but there are exceptions.

### Usually masculine (der)
- Days, months and seasons: der Montag, der Januar, der Sommer
- Weather and directions: der Regen, der Schnee, der Norden
- Nouns ending in -er for male people and tools: der Lehrer, der Computer
- Endings -ling, -ismus, -ist, -or: der Frühling, der Tourismus, der Polizist, der Motor
- Car brands and alcoholic drinks: der BMW, der Wein (exception: das Bier)

### Usually feminine (die)
- Endings -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -ion, -tät, -ik, -ie: die Zeitung, die Freiheit, die Möglichkeit, die Freundschaft, die Information, die Universität, die Musik, die Energie
- Most nouns ending in -e: die Straße, die Blume (exceptions: der Name, der Käse, das Auge)
- Female people ending in -in: die Lehrerin, die Ärztin
- Numbers as nouns: die Eins, die Zwei

### Usually neuter (das)
- Diminutives ending in -chen and -lein: das Mädchen (girl), das Häuschen, das Fräulein
- Infinitives used as nouns: das Essen, das Schwimmen, das Lernen
- Endings -um, -ment, -ma: das Museum, das Dokument, das Thema
- Most metals and chemical elements: das Gold, das Eisen
- Young animals and people: das Kind, das Baby, das Kalb

## Compound nouns

In compound nouns, the gender comes from the LAST word:
- der Tisch + die Lampe = die Tischlampe (desk lamp)
- die Hand + der Schuh = der Handschuh (glove)
- das Haus + die Tür = die Haustür (front door)

## Common mistakes

- "Das Mädchen" is neuter even though it means girl, because of the -chen ending.
- "Die Person" is always feminine, even for a man.
- "Der See" means lake, but "die See" means sea. Some nouns change meaning with gender: der Leiter (leader) / die Leiter (ladder), das Band (ribbon) / die Band (music band).
